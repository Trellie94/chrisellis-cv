My role

From planning drawings to a staged show flat

On every Nutbrook development scheme I ran the project once planning was in hand, through to practical completion and sales staging.

  1. Design

    Value-engineered the planning layouts for design and buildability. Appointed and managed architects, structural, energy and HVAC engineers and interior designers to a full detailed design pack.

  2. Procure

    Ran the main-contractor tender and appointment. Let selected packages direct to subcontractors.

  3. Build

    Managed the main contractor through construction. Discharged planning conditions with the architects and led Building Control inspections.

  4. Fund

    Prepared senior-lender drawdowns with supporting evidence and hosted the monitoring QS. Made all contractor and staff payments.

  5. Complete

    Took the interior spec in-house, drew lighting and electrical layouts, ordered all second-fix and furnished the homes for sale.

My business partner sourced sites, ran planning with our consultant, and handled pricing, agents and conveyancing. We appraised every site together.
