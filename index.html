01 · The first flat

An HVAC engineer with a studio flat to refurbish

I was working as an HVAC engineer in London when my brothers and I bought a studio flat in Earlsfield to refurbish. They wanted to invest in the UK. I wanted to build something with my own hands.

When it was finished I left my job and went into construction full time.

02 · Builder for hire

Running other people's builds

With my co-founder Mark Baracas, I ran Nutbrook as a small construction business: freelance project management and white-label contracting on refurbishments, extensions and loft conversions.

Radipole Road · Weiss Road · Hydethorpe Road · Ebner Road · Cedars Road

03 · A derelict pub

Nine hotel rooms and a basement bar

With my brothers again, in Cumbria. We bought the Tithe Barn in Cockermouth as a derelict pub, stripped it back to the stone and rebuilt it as a 9-room boutique hotel with a basement bar.

We kept the original staircase and the stained glass, and rebuilt around them.

Also in Cumbria · Old Mill Inn, Maryport · Highland Drove · Cross Keys

04 · Developing with a partner

Four developments alongside a developer ten years ahead of us

Nutbrook then moved into development. Mark and I did our first four schemes as a joint venture with Handy Heroes, who had ten years of development experience.

20 units across four developments, three of them with basement excavations.

49 Selkirk Road · 15 Smallwood Road · 162 Mitcham Road · 58 Selkirk Road

05 · On our own

Finding the sites, raising the finance, delivering the flats

The next four developments were Nutbrook's alone. We found the sites, raised the finance and delivered them without a partner.

23 flats delivered as our own developer. £6.8m of private investment into Nutbrook, all of it returned.

28 Trinity Road · 4 Aldrington Road · 1 Deburgh Road · 129 Lower Richmond Road

06 · Our own construction team

Developer and contractor on the same scheme

For the last two developments I built an in-house construction team. I hired a foreman and a project manager, and we ran everything ourselves, from procurement to site labour.

12 flats and over £2.5m of development cost, excluding land, delivered under Nutbrook Construction.
